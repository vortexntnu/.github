# Vortex NTNU

Vortex NTNU is a student organization at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) that designs and builds autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs).

We develop our vehicles to solve challenging autonomous underwater tasks and compete internationally in competitions such as **RoboSub** and the **TAC Challenge**.

Our goal is to give students hands-on experience developing real engineering systems while pushing the capabilities of autonomous underwater vehicles.

Previous Vortex vehicles include **Manta**, **Beluga**, **Freya**, and **Orca**.
